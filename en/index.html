Delivery scope

Warehouse / terminal → destination terminal. No door-to-door, DDP or destination customs clearance.

For Iran, the buyer clears at Sarakhs or Lotfabad.

The 0.4% Kyrgyzstan tax applies only to Wuqia / Irkeshtam shipments routed through Kyrgyzstan. It is paid by the customer and does not apply to Khorgos.

TIR

Service options

TIR is available. The vehicle is sealed at origin and can reduce transloads, but does not eliminate them. TIR does not change transit time or price.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

All FAQs
Which countries do you serve?

Iran, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Uzbekistan, Tajikistan and Turkmenistan. Central Asian routing and transit time are provided with the formal quote.

Which border gateways do you use?

Wuqia / Irkeshtam and Khorgos.

What are the transit times to Iran?

Published ranges apply only to Wuqia / Irkeshtam. Khorgos transit time is provided with quote.

What is the difference between Direct and Transload?

Direct means one transload in Bukhara. Transload means Osh and Bukhara, or at the Iran border.
