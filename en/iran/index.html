Iran Road Freight from Xinjiang

Choose the Wuqia / Irkeshtam or Khorgos gateway. The transit ranges below apply only to Wuqia.

Wuqia / Irkeshtam

  1. 01Xinjiang
  2. 02Kyrgyzstan
  3. 03Uzbekistan
  4. 04Turkmenistan
  5. 05Iran

Via Kyrgyzstan. Direct means one transload in Bukhara. Transload means Osh and Bukhara, or at the Iran border.

Khorgos

  1. 01Xinjiang
  2. 02Kazakhstan
  3. 03Uzbekistan
  4. 04Turkmenistan
  5. 05Iran

Via Kazakhstan. Transit time is provided with quote. The Kyrgyzstan 0.4% tax does not apply to this line.

Equipment & delivery scope

Tilt / curtain-side semi-trailers, up to 96 m³ and 25 tons per truck; general cargo, and lithium batteries (UN 3480 / 3481) declared with a 16-section MSDS and UN packaging documents and confirmed per shipment. Terminal-to-terminal: the buyer clears at Sarakhs or Lotfabad. CPT / DAP option: delivery to Tehran West Customs, Mashhad Customs or Aprin Dry Port bonded warehouse, where the consignee’s broker clears. No door-to-door, DDP or destination clearance.

Tax note

The customer pays the 0.4% Kyrgyzstan tax only when a Wuqia / Irkeshtam shipment is routed through Kyrgyzstan. It does not apply to Khorgos.
