Full trucks from Xinjiang: get the truck count right

On this page
  1. Full trucks, not part loads
  2. Check volume and weight together
  3. The cargo-ready date
  4. Gateway and destination
  5. FAQ

CargoIranTruck enquiries are for full trucks from Xinjiang. Before asking for a rate, state the proposed number of trucks and the cargo-ready date. Give both volume and weight: each truck is limited to 96 CBM and 25 t, and anything beyond that is reviewed separately.

  • This site handles full-truck enquiries only
  • Per truck: up to 96 CBM and 25 t
  • State truck count and cargo-ready date
  • Formal quotes come by email with a validity date

Full trucks, not part loads

This page is for buyers or exporters who need one or more full trucks. Part-load or consolidated enquiries are not accepted on this site. If you are not sure whether the goods fill one truck, send package dimensions, package count and gross weight instead of guessing.

Check volume and weight together

Dense cargo can reach the weight limit before it fills the trailer; lighter cargo can fill the volume first. State both cubic metres and gross kilograms. If the plan exceeds 96 CBM or 25 t, the split between trucks or the over-limit part must be reviewed separately.

The cargo-ready date

Give the date the goods are actually ready to load, not only the factory order date. If part of the cargo will be ready later, list it separately. Put product description, HS code, volume, weight and truck count in the same email so later revisions are not confused with the original figures.

Gateway and destination

Name Wuqia or Khorgos, or say the gateway is still open. For Iran, specify the destination customs point; the delivery method must be clear in the enquiry. The buyer handles Iran clearance; the service is not door-to-door or DDP. We publish no Khorgos day count. The Kyrgyz 0.4% applies only on the Wuqia line and is a tax, not freight.

FAQ

Does this site accept part loads?

No. Enquiries on this site are for full trucks only.

What if the cargo is heavy but compact?

Both limits apply at the same time. Reaching 25 t fills the weight capacity even when volume is below 96 CBM.

Should I choose the number of trucks myself?

Give your estimate, together with package dimensions and weights, so the loading plan can be checked.
