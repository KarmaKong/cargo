Starting in Xinjiang: two road corridors to Iran and Central Asia

On this page

  2. Times and the 0.4%

This site is the Xinjiang origin product. Trucks do not start at the factory door. We take cargo at a warehouse / yard and deliver to a destination yard. No door-to-door. No DDP.

  • Wuqia: Xinjiang → Kyrgyzstan → Uzbekistan → Turkmenistan → Iran
  • Khorgos: Xinjiang → Kazakhstan → Uzbekistan → Turkmenistan → Iran
  • Published times apply only to Wuqia, counted from the border; no days for Khorgos
  • The 0.4% Kyrgyz transit tax is Wuqia only; a tax, not freight

Two corridors from Xinjiang

Turugart and Alashankou are not in use. Equipment is a tilt / curtain-side trailer, not a container. Iranian customs entry is Sarakhs or Lotfabad. The buyer clears; we do not.

TIR means fewer openings and truck changes, not none. It does not change time or freight. There is no public live tracking; nodes are updated by hand.

Times and the 0.4%

Published times apply only to Wuqia and are counted from the border (as of 2026-09-07): Sarakhs and Lotfabad direct 15–18 days, transshipment 21–24; Mashhad direct 18–21, transshipment 24–28; Tehran direct 21–23, transshipment 26–30. We do not publish days for Khorgos.

On Wuqia there is a Kyrgyz transit tax of 0.4%. It is a tax, not freight; it is not in the quote; the customer pays it separately. Khorgos does not go through Kyrgyzstan, so this item does not apply. We do not state the tax base here.

No rates on the page. Email the Xinjiang origin yard, destination yard, cargo description, gross weight and volume for a written quote with a validity date.

FAQ

Do trucks start at the factory door?

No. Warehouse / yard to destination yard. No door-to-door. No DDP.

Is Tajikistan a destination on these lines?

The two corridors on sale run via Kyrgyzstan or Kazakhstan, then Uzbekistan and Turkmenistan to Iran. Tajikistan is not a published destination on those two lines.

How many days for Khorgos?

We do not publish days. The table is Wuqia only.

Is the 0.4% freight?

No. It is a Kyrgyz transit tax on Wuqia only, excluded from the quote, paid by the customer. Khorgos does not have it.
