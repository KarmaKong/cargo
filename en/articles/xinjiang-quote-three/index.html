Three fields before a Xinjiang written quote

On this page
  1. Three fields, not a weight
  2. A sellable destination, not only a border
  3. What the letter does not include
  4. FAQ

Cargo leaves from Xinjiang. A written rate needs three fields together. Wuqia or Khorgos is the exit choice; this page is the enquiry itself.

  • Origin hub in Xinjiang
  • Destination customs: Mashhad or Tehran West
  • Cargo data: HS, gross weight, CBM
  • Missing one, no written quote

Three fields, not a weight

The origin hub is Yiwu, Shenzhen, Guangzhou or a supervised yard at the exit — not the factory. Cargo data is HS, kilograms gross and cubic metres. Ceiling 96 CBM and 25 T per vehicle; over-limit is separate. This route is full-truck (FTL) only; we do not accept LCL.

A sellable destination, not only a border

For Iran, sellable destinations are Mashhad Customs or Tehran West Customs. Entry is Sarakhs. The buyer clears. Hub to customs, CPT / DAP. Not door-to-door, not DDP. Tajikistan is not a destination on these two lines. For a mid-corridor drop, name the yard.

What the letter does not include

No rates on the page. Quotes have a validity date. Days are Wuqia from the border only, currently Mashhad 18–21 direct / 24–28 transshipment and Tehran 21–23 / 26–30. No Khorgos day count. The Kyrgyz 0.4% is Wuqia only — a tax, not freight. Lithium on line one with a 16-section MSDS and UN packing.

FAQ

Is weight enough?

No. Origin hub, destination customs, and cargo data are all required.

Can the destination be Sarakhs?

Sarakhs is the entry. Sellable destinations are Mashhad Customs or Tehran West Customs.

Are rates on the site?

No. Dated email quote.
